How to read a pop-up story

Turn the page

Use the page corners, your arrow keys, or swipe on a phone. Each page lifts at the spine and turns over.

Pull the tabs

Some pages hide a paper tab. Pull it to move part of the scene, like flying higher or rowing closer.

Watch it fold

Every scene is built from layers of cut paper that rise as a page opens and fold flat as it closes.
